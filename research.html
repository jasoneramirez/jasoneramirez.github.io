Journal Articles

Mathematical optimization modelling for group counterfactual explanations

E. Carrizosa, J. Ramírez-Ayerbe, D. Romero Morales · European Journal of Operational Research, 319(2):399–412, 2024

A mathematical optimization framework for generating counterfactual explanations at the group level. Moving beyond individual explanations, we adopt a stakeholder perspective in which counterfactuals are produced jointly under structural and feasibility constraints, revealing close connections with classical facility location problems. Awarded the SEIO–Fundación BBVA Award 2024.

Counterfactual analysis and target setting in benchmarking

P. Bogetoft, J. Ramírez-Ayerbe, D. Romero Morales · European Journal of Operational Research, 315(3):1083–1095, 2024

We extend counterfactual reasoning to Data Envelopment Analysis (DEA), introducing an optimization-based framework for interpretable target setting in efficiency analysis. Counterfactual targets are computed via mixed-integer convex optimization, providing transparent and actionable explanations for inefficient units.

Generating collective counterfactual explanations in score-based classification via mathematical optimization

E. Carrizosa, J. Ramírez-Ayerbe, D. Romero Morales · Expert Systems with Applications, 238:121954, 2024

Collective counterfactual explanations generated for an entire group of individuals at once. We formulate the problem as a mixed-integer model that minimizes total perturbation cost while guaranteeing a favorable outcome, allowing shared influential features and outliers to be identified simultaneously.

A new model for counterfactual analysis for functional data

E. Carrizosa, J. Ramírez-Ayerbe, D. Romero Morales · Advances in Data Analysis and Classification, 18:981–1000, 2024

A counterfactual analysis framework tailored to functional data, where instances are curves rather than vectors. The approach yields interpretable, optimization-based explanations that respect the functional structure of the inputs.
